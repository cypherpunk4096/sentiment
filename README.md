```
An instrument is *direct*    if it elicits stated beliefs           (surveys)
              *revealed*  if it infers disposition from costly behavior
                                                (flows, options, on-chain transfers)
              *expressive* if it infers disposition from communicative behavior
                                                (news text, posts, search queries)
```

## The three movements

**First**, the theoretical quarrel that made sentiment measurable at all: efficient markets
defining sentiment as noise destined for elimination, against the noise-trader and behavioral
traditions showing the noise is priced, persistent, and systematic. **Second**, the modern
toolkit — Michigan surveys, the closed-end fund discount, the VIX, Baker–Wurgler, media- and
search-based measures, the Fear & Greed composites — each evaluated by what it actually
observes. **Third**, the emotonomic turn: instruments native to a system where feeling is a
constituent of value.

## The Fear & Greed index

The practitioner composite the paper treats at length: the
[CNN Fear & Greed Index](https://www.cnn.com/markets/fear-and-greed) (equities — seven
revealed inputs: momentum, strength, breadth, put/call, junk demand, volatility, haven
demand) and the [Crypto Fear & Greed Index](https://alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/)
(volatility, momentum/volume, social, dominance, trends). Both are *revealed-and-expressed*
composites read on a 0–100 dial — the received form the emotonomic instruments extend: where
Fear & Greed reads disposition *toward risk*, the gesture-based dial reads disposition
*toward each other*.

## The native instruments

Gesture velocity, resonance depth, emotional reciprocity — formalized as on-chain *revealed*
measures — composed into a sentiment instrument for an on-chain emotional economy, clocked in
blocktime normalized by average blocktime (never wall time), including the return-from-ping
interval. Sentiment is what the community verifiably *does* on-chain, never self-report.
The paper derives testable propositions and answers the strongest objections, including
Goodhart drift, reflexivity, and thin-market pathology.
